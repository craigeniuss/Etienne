    FAQ

    Answers Before You Visit

    Clear answers reduce uncertainty — the most common questions from lot buyers, answered upfront.

    Still Have Questions? Book a Tour
    Who is the developer?

    Étienne at Deer Creek Meadows is managed and owned by Scott Lybbert, with land planning and engineering by Berg Engineering.

    Will adjacent open space be developed?

    HOA open spaces are designated and recorded in county records — they cannot be developed.

    What school district serves the community?

    Wasatch County School District.

    What is the pet and livestock policy?

    Family pets are permitted. Dogs must be leashed in public spaces. Livestock is not permitted.

    Are private pools allowed?

    Yes — private pools are permitted under community design guidelines.

    Who maintains roads and snow removal?

    All community roads are maintained by the HOA, including snow removal. Driveways are the homeowner's responsibility.

    Are rentals allowed?

    Yes — rentals are permitted with a 5-day minimum stay.
